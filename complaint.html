Rules
▍ Logging Guidelines
  • Log complaints honestly — mark as open or closed.
  • For ongoing issues, leave Stop Time blank.
  • Downtime calculates automatically from start → stop.
  • ✎ Edit any open complaint to add the stop time and action taken (closes it).
  • Your own complaints: edit or 🗑 remove for 7 days. Removed ones can be restored. After that, ask an admin.
  • Tap a complaint to see its full details and change history.
  • Only admin can add new areas to the dropdown.
